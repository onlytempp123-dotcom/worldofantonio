Code of Conduct

Community Rules

To keep WorldOfAntonio welcoming, safe, and enjoyable for everyone across the globe, all members agree to follow these core principles.

01

Don't Abuse

Zero tolerance for personal attacks, harassment, hate speech, slurs, or toxic hostility toward any member.

02

Follow Admins

Respect channel operators (@ops) and network admins. Their moderation decisions keep the network stable and fair.

03

Don't Spam

Keep the chat readable. No message flooding, excessive CAPS, ASCII walls, automated bots, or unsolicited self-promotion.

04

No Negative Groupism

No toxic cliques, gatekeeping, brigading, or ganging up on newcomers. Everyone belongs in the conversation equally.

05

Respect Everyone

Treat fellow members from every corner of the world with kindness, empathy, and basic human dignity—even when debating.

06

Keep It Chill & Constructive

Bring good energy, respect channel topics, and help cultivate an inspiring space where people love hanging out.

07

Adults Only (18+)

This is a mature community. Participation in WorldOfAntonio and our Web IRC Client is strictly restricted to individuals aged 18 and older.

08

Authorized Bots Only

Automated scripts and custom bots require prior approval. Please contact the admin team before connecting any bot to the chat.
